# Researcher protocol and answer key

Evaluation question: Does selecting calendar dates change accuracy or completion time when people compare changes in parking availability across dates?

Hypothesis: calendar selection reduces completion time without reducing accuracy. The static layout may also help simultaneous comparisons; no outcome is assumed.

Every participant uses both conditions and receives two different task variants in each. Set A contains T1A/T2A; Set B contains T1B/T2B. Condition/set allocation, timing, and scoring are defined in README.md and implemented by the website.

## Questions

T1A: Compare the gym on October 7 and October 9, 2026. From the first recorded visit to the last recorded visit that day, which date had the larger increase in percentage available?

Answer: October 7. Its accessible-space count rises from 8 to 25 (+50.0 percentage points); October 9 rises from 17 to 25 (+23.5 points), with capacity 34.

T1B: Compare the gym on October 12 and October 14, 2026. From the first recorded visit to the last recorded visit that day, which date had the larger increase in percentage available?

Answer: October 14. October 12 rises from 4 to 19 (+44.1 points); October 14 rises from 5 to 27 (+64.7 points).

T2A: Compare school on October 22 and October 23, 2026. From the first recorded visit that day to the lowest recorded availability between 09:00 and 16:00 inclusive, which date had the larger decrease in percentage available?

Answer: October 22. It decreases from 31 to 1 (−66.7 points); October 23 decreases from 24 to 2 (−48.9 points), with capacity 45.

T2B: Compare school on October 26 and October 27, 2026. From the first recorded visit that day to the lowest recorded availability between 09:00 and 16:00 inclusive, which date had the larger decrease in percentage available?

Answer: October 26. It decreases from 37 to 0 (−82.2 points); October 27 decreases from 24 to 0 (−53.3 points).

These questions compare changes across dates, not a single maximum. Answers were verified using the same 159 observations supplied to both web conditions.

## Pilot and main study

Conduct the pilot with one person and preserve their files separately from the main ten. Check that participants understand the comparison wording and time interval, can read both views, complete tasks within three minutes, and copy results and paste them into the submission form. Observe or ask about any problems during pilot only. Document all changes before the main study. The website cannot verify whether the pilot occurred; the researcher is responsible for this requirement.

Recruit at least ten other people who are not members of the project group. Each completes all four tasks, providing at least forty genuine main-study measurements. Only send main invitations after the pilot is complete. Freeze the website and questions for the main study.
